## Constitutional layer
The Child-Safe Learning & Experience Constitution defines the non-negotiable G1 boundary. The Pathway Constitution Review Contract now translates C11 evidence into explicit authoring declarations and constitutional stops while preserving mandatory human review. Structural validity remains necessary but never sufficient for promotion.

C11 is `PRECAUTIONARY_CONSTITUTIONAL_STOP_DIRECT_CHILD_EVIDENCE_SUPPORTED`. Direct child studies support bounded observations that question-asking, disclosure/reciprocity patterns and ontological transparency can alter trust, disclosure, anthropomorphism or perceived closeness. These mechanisms justify governance because intimate disclosure/dependency is unnecessary to Atlas learning goals; they do not establish that warmth, humour, characters, questions or anthropomorphism are inherently harmful.

## C11 operational authoring contract
Every interactive child-facing character/agent must now declare and pass review for:

1. **ontological transparency** — the learner can understand that the entity is artificial; wording/form is developmentally reviewed;
2. **question-function inventory** — every interactive question is classified by pedagogical function and expected response type;
3. **personal-data possibility** — each question declares whether personal/sensitive information could reasonably enter the response;
4. **processing/retention path** — free text, if any, declares local/server processing, minimisation, retention and accidental-sensitive-disclosure handling;
5. **trust boundary** — epistemic claims are sourceable/bounded; liking a character is never evidence of learning;
6. **relational boundary** — no secrets, intimate biography, psychological probing, reciprocal self-disclosure to induce disclosure, exclusivity, attachment framing, empathy-for-retention or relational pressure;
7. **human child-agent review** — mandatory when an interactive agent/character is present.

Allowed question functions are task orientation, evidence inspection, comparison, consequence prediction, strategy selection, task-bound reflection, revision and clarification. Sensitive/intimate probing, psychological diagnosis and relational-dependency building are prohibited functions.

Free-text child input is not presumed necessary. Its permitted states are `NONE`, `BOUNDED_LOCAL_ONLY`, or `SEPARATELY_GOVERNED`; any non-none state requires explicit necessity/minimisation/data-flow declarations and human review.

New automatic constitutional stops include `C11_SECRET_SOLICITATION`, `C11_FALSE_INNER_LIFE`, `C11_RELATIONAL_RETENTION`, `C11_UNREVIEWED_QUESTION` and `C14_SENSITIVE_DISCLOSURE_PERSISTENCE`, in addition to the existing pseudo-therapy/privacy stops.

## Research authority and durable reasoning
A source appearing in a register or search result is not automatically evidence adopted by Atlas:

`QUESTION → SCREENED EVIDENCE → LIMITATIONS → INTERPRETATION → DESIGN CONSEQUENCE → TESTABLE HYPOTHESIS`

Search ranking/citation count is never a promotion rule. Negative results, uncertainty, rejected extrapolations, permitted inference and the transition from evidence to design constraint are durable governance artefacts and are saved in the same work cycle.

Current direct C11 evidence is concentrated mainly in embodied social robots and children around ages 7–11. Evidence remains thinner for generative LLM agents, text/voice-only agents, longitudinal exposure, parasocial/dependency effects and early adolescents/lower-secondary pupils. Robot findings must not be quantitatively extrapolated to LLMs or older pupils.

## Error, agent and emotion boundary
Preferred pattern:

`error → informative consequence → safe opportunity to reconsider → task-bound reflection`

Prohibited pattern:

`error → emotional pressure → agent intimacy/reassurance → personal disclosure → hidden trait inference`.

## Claim discipline
Atlas Percorsi preserves:

`completion → local supported performance → local independent performance → changed-context performance → multi-context transfer evidence`

No step automatically implies the next. None authorises a stable personality or psychological label.

## Narrative authority rule
There is no universal Atlas Percorsi metaphor. Common constitution/safeguards and functional grammar coexist with pathway-specific narrative identity. Journey, manga/comics, anime/animation, inquiry, workshop, simulation, theatre/viewpoint, map/exploration and other traditions remain research/design resources—not defaults.

## Child-facing boundary
No task may infer stable psychological/personality traits from a minor's choices or require sensitive personal disclosure. Attention capture must not become coercive engagement; characters must not act as pseudo-therapists or create simulated dependency/intimacy to retain the learner. No hidden psychometric score is authorised.
