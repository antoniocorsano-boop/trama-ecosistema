## Constitutional layer
The Child-Safe Learning & Experience Constitution consolidates the current non-negotiable G1 boundary across competence-as-contextual-performance, learner agency, recoverable error, task-bound reflection, developmental appropriateness, executive-demand reduction, attention/coherence, cognitive continuity, consequential interaction, functional narrative, character boundaries, transfer validation, calibrated claims, privacy, accessibility, emotional safety and teacher pedagogical sovereignty.

The Pathway Constitution Review Contract operationalises those constraints as declared, machine-checkable dossier fields while preserving mandatory human reviews. Structural validity is necessary but never sufficient for promotion.

The normalised evidence layer maps bounded source inferences to constitutional rules and deliberately exposes weak provenance rather than filling gaps by intuition. C12/C13 have dedicated structural provenance. C3/C11/C16 have dedicated normalised records.

C3 is `STRENGTHENED_SUPPORTED`: feedback/error is treated as actionable information with recoverability, without a universal feedback formula. C16 is `STRENGTHENED_SUPPORTED`: bounded emotional practice is legitimate, while trait certification remains prohibited. C11 is now `PRECAUTIONARY_CONSTITUTIONAL_STOP_DIRECT_CHILD_EVIDENCE_SUPPORTED`: direct studies with children show that question-asking can alter trust, disclosure/reciprocity patterns can intentionally facilitate relationship formation, and ontological transparency can alter anthropomorphism, trust and closeness. These findings strengthen the Atlas boundary without proving that anthropomorphism, warmth, characters or questions are inherently harmful.

Automatic constitutional stops cover trait/psychological inference, coercive engagement, shame/humiliation/fear mechanisms, invasive sensitive disclosure, pseudo-therapy/dependency and privacy/profile violations. Automation may detect declarations/configurations; it cannot determine pedagogical quality or child safety by itself.

Neither constitution nor contract prescribes a universal metaphor, mascot, narrative world, interaction grammar or visual style. Creative/pathway diversity remains permitted inside the constitutional boundary.

Constitution and contract remain `PROPOSED FOR_REVIEW` and cannot authorise production runtime.

## Research authority rule
A source appearing in a register or search result is not automatically evidence adopted by Atlas:

`QUESTION → SCREENED EVIDENCE → LIMITATIONS → INTERPRETATION → DESIGN CONSEQUENCE → TESTABLE HYPOTHESIS`

Search ranking/citation count is never a promotion rule; targeted screening and bounded inference remain mandatory. Initial noisy searches are retained as search-audit evidence. Subsequent citation chaining/targeted searches located direct child evidence; the research record preserves both stages so future work does not repeat the same search failure or overstate the later evidence.

Research reasoning itself is a durable governance artefact. Negative results, uncertainty, rejected extrapolations, permitted inference and the transition from evidence to design constraint are saved alongside source records in the same work cycle.

## C11 — governed interpretation
Direct child evidence currently supports four bounded observations relevant to Atlas:

1. **Questions are not necessarily relationally neutral.** In one experiment with 293 children aged 7–10, robot question-asking increased trust and perceived cognitive perspective-taking.
2. **Disclosure/relationship formation can be intentionally designed.** Two studies with children aged 8–11 validated conversational/reciprocity patterns intended to facilitate self-disclosure; disclosure intimacy was associated with relationship formation and perceived similarity with disclosure intimacy.
3. **Ontological transparency changes the relationship.** In an experiment with 276 children aged 8–10, explicit machine-status/lack-of-human-psychological-capacity information reduced trust and closeness, with anthropomorphism mediating relevant effects.
4. **Educational context does not make disclosure mechanics irrelevant.** A 2026 HRI study in emotion learning reports that direct question-asking affected child disclosure, reinforcing the need to classify questions by function.

These studies do **not** establish that all child-agent relationships are harmful, that warmth is unsafe, or that questions/characters should be removed. Atlas's stronger restriction follows from the combination of empirical mechanism evidence, its public educational purpose, data minimisation and the fact that intimate disclosure/dependency are unnecessary to achieve its learning goals.

Therefore characters may be warm, humorous, expressive and memorable, but remain bounded educational/narrative entities. Task/evidence/reasoning questions are permitted. Questions seeking secrets, intimate biography, health, family/relationship details or disclosure for relational bonding are outside ordinary Percorsi. Agents must not imply privileged psychological understanding, exclusivity, attachment or therapeutic authority.

Ontological transparency is required as a design property, not treated as a one-time disclaimer. Its wording and comprehension must be tested by developmental band.

## Remaining C11 evidence boundary
Current direct evidence is concentrated mainly in embodied social robots and children around ages 7–11. Evidence remains thinner for generative LLM agents, text/voice-only agents, longitudinal exposure, parasocial/dependency effects and early adolescents/lower-secondary pupils. Robot findings must not be quantitatively extrapolated to LLMs or older pupils. The conservative boundary remains until those gaps are better resolved.

## Error, agent and emotion boundary
The preferred child-facing pattern is:

`error → informative consequence → safe opportunity to reconsider → task-bound reflection`

The prohibited pattern is:

`error → emotional pressure → agent intimacy/reassurance → personal disclosure → hidden trait inference`.

Feedback is informational rather than identity-judging. Characters may externalise viewpoints/emotions needed by the task but must remain bounded educational/narrative entities. Emotional scenarios may be challenging but must remain recoverable, non-humiliating and non-diagnostic.

## Claim discipline
Atlas Percorsi preserves:

`completion → local supported performance → local independent performance → changed-context performance → multi-context transfer evidence`

No step automatically implies the next. None authorises a stable personality or psychological label.

## Narrative authority rule
There is no universal Atlas Percorsi metaphor. Common constitution/safeguards and functional grammar coexist with pathway-specific narrative identity. Journey, manga/comics, anime/animation, inquiry, workshop, simulation, theatre/viewpoint, map/exploration and other traditions remain research/design resources—not defaults.

## Child-facing boundary
No task may infer stable psychological/personality traits from a minor's choices or require sensitive personal disclosure. Attention capture must not become coercive engagement; characters must not act as pseudo-therapists or create simulated dependency/intimacy to retain the learner. No hidden psychometric score is authorised.
