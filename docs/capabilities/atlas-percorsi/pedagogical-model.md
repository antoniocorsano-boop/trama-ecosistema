# CAP-ATLAS-PERCORSI — Pedagogical Model

**Gate:** G1 — Discovery  
**Status:** WORKING_HYPOTHESIS  
**Approval:** NONE

## Purpose

Preserve the pedagogical reasoning developed so far while keeping evidence, hypotheses and future decisions distinguishable.

## Core working hypothesis

Transversal competence should not be presented as isolated content to consume. The experience should repeatedly connect awareness, choice, action, reflection and transfer.

Candidate cycle:

`SELF-AWARENESS → CHOICE → ACTION → OBSERVATION → REFLECTION → TRANSFER`

The cycle is not yet an approved specification.

## Candidate progression territories

1. **Me** — self-awareness, interests, emotions, strengths, difficulties, strategies;
2. **Me as a learner** — metacognition, attention, organisation, persistence, strategy selection;
3. **Me and others** — listening, communication, collaboration, empathy, conflict management;
4. **Me facing problems** — critical thinking, creativity, decision making, uncertainty and problem solving;
5. **Me in the world** — responsibility, citizenship, sustainability, digital/information participation;
6. **Me as a planner/agent** — goals, initiative, orientation and turning intention into action.

These territories require evidence/framework mapping and age progression before adoption.

## Candidate experience grammar

`SITUATION → CHOICE → EXPERIENCE/ACTION → EVIDENCE → REFLECTION → NEW CONTEXT`

The transfer step is essential: completing an activity is not equivalent to demonstrating transferable competence.

## Candidate formats

- **Trigger / Scintilla:** short experience that raises a question or awareness;
- **Challenge / Sfida:** bounded experience that exercises a competence;
- **Pathway / Percorso:** sequence of experiences supporting progression and transfer.

Names and durations are not frozen.

## Progress representation

Current direction: evidence/progression rather than numerical identity scoring.

Candidate language of progression:

- experienced;
- beginning to recognise;
- uses with support;
- uses independently;
- chooses when to use;
- transfers to a new situation.

This must be tested for developmental appropriateness, accessibility and compatibility with applicable competence frameworks.

## Teacher and learner roles

Working direction:

- Atlas can be accessed publicly by the learner;
- the learner retains agency over personal reflection;
- teacher mediation may be appropriate for selected experiences but should not convert Atlas into student surveillance;
- connections to curriculum/materials may be surfaced without assigning a transversal competence exclusively to one subject;
- any Docente OS planning relationship must be governed at G2.

## Explicit pedagogical boundaries

Do not conflate:

- competence development with personality measurement;
- reflection with psychological diagnosis;
- engagement with manipulation;
- completion with competence;
- personalisation with personal-data profiling;
- gamification with ranking/social comparison.

## Evidence still required

See `research-evidence-register.md`. G1 cannot PASS until the model is mapped to stable scholarly and authoritative evidence, relevant frameworks, age progression, inclusion/accessibility and documented alternatives.
